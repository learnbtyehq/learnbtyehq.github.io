What Is SEO?

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. It helps websites appear better in search engine results.

How Does SEO Work?

SEO improves a website through useful content, proper keywords, good structure, and a better user experience.

Why Is SEO Important?

Good SEO can help people discover a website through search engines and bring more relevant visitors.

Conclusion

SEO is a long-term process. Creating useful content and maintaining a well-structured website are important for better visibility.
