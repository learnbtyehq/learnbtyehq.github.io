What Is SEO? A Beginner's Guide

Learn what search engine optimization means, how search engines discover and understand websites, and the basic SEO practices beginners should know.

SEO Guide

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. It is the process of improving webpages so search engines can understand their content and users can find useful information through search results.

SEO is not simply about adding keywords to a page. Good SEO combines useful content, clear page structure, crawlable links, descriptive titles, relevant internal links and a website that works well for users.

What Does SEO Mean?

Search Engine Optimization is a collection of practices used to help webpages become easier for search engines and people to discover and understand.

Search engines such as Google use automated systems to discover webpages, understand their content and determine which results may be useful for a particular search.

How Does SEO Work?

A simple way to understand SEO is to think about three important stages:

  1. Crawling: Search engines discover webpages by following links and using other discovery methods.
  2. Indexing: Search engines analyze and store information about pages that they are able to index.
  3. Ranking: When someone searches, search systems determine which relevant results should be shown and in what order.

Why Is SEO Important?

Search can help people discover websites, businesses, educational resources and useful information. A well-optimized website makes its content easier to understand and discover.

SEO can also improve the structure and usability of a website because many good SEO practices overlap with good website organization.

Main Types of SEO

On-Page SEO

On-page SEO focuses on elements that exist directly on a webpage, including the title, headings, content, URLs, images and internal links.

Learn more: On-Page SEO Checklist

Technical SEO

Technical SEO focuses on technical aspects that help search engines access and understand a website. Examples include crawlability, indexability, canonical URLs, redirects, structured data and site architecture.

Learn more: Technical SEO Checklist

Off-Page SEO

Off-page SEO generally refers to signals and activities that happen outside your own website. Links from other relevant websites are one important example.

SEO and Keywords

Keywords are words and phrases people use when searching for information. Understanding the language and questions of your audience can help you create content that matches their needs.

However, keywords should be used naturally. Repeating a keyword excessively does not make content better.

SEO Best Practices for Beginners

  • Create useful content for real users.
  • Use clear and descriptive page titles.
  • Organize content with meaningful headings.
  • Use descriptive URLs.
  • Connect related pages with useful internal links.
  • Optimize images without sacrificing useful visual information.
  • Make important pages accessible to search engines.
  • Keep important technical information accurate and up to date.

Common SEO Mistakes

  • Writing only for search engines instead of people.
  • Keyword stuffing.
  • Creating many pages with little useful information.
  • Ignoring broken internal links.
  • Using unclear page titles.
  • Publishing outdated information without reviewing it.

How to Start Learning SEO

Beginners should first understand how crawling, indexing and search results work. After that, learn on-page SEO and technical SEO before moving into more advanced subjects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SEO free?

SEO itself does not require paying a search engine to appear in organic search results. However, websites may spend money on tools, content, development or professional SEO services.

How long does SEO take?

SEO results vary depending on the website, competition, content quality, technical condition and many other factors. There is no guaranteed ranking timeframe.

Is SEO only about Google?

No. SEO can apply to different search engines and search systems. Google is one of the largest search engines, so many SEO discussions focus on Google Search.

Conclusion

SEO is about making useful content easier for search engines to discover, understand and present to people. Start with a technically accessible website, create genuinely useful content and organize related information clearly.
