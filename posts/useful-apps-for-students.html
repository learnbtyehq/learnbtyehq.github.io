10 Useful Apps Every Student Should Know

From taking notes and organizing assignments to storing files and improving productivity, the right apps can make everyday student tasks easier.

Technology Guide

Students use smartphones, tablets and computers for much more than entertainment. The right apps can help with studying, taking notes, managing assignments, storing files and staying organized.

You do not need dozens of apps to study effectively. A small collection of useful tools can cover most everyday academic needs.

1. Google Drive

Google Drive is useful for storing and organizing documents, PDFs, images and other files in the cloud. It also works with Google Docs, Sheets and Slides, making it useful for students who work across multiple devices.

A major advantage is that files can be accessed from a phone, tablet or computer, which makes it convenient when moving between home, school and college.

2. Google Docs

Google Docs is useful for writing assignments, project reports, notes and other documents. Documents can be edited from different devices and shared with other people for collaboration.

It is especially useful for group projects where multiple students need to work on the same document.

3. Google Classroom

Google Classroom is designed to help students and teachers manage classes, assignments and class communication.

Students can view assignments and class materials, while teachers can distribute and review work through the platform.

[Google Classroom on Google Play](https://reference-url-citation.invalid/2)

4. Google Keep

Google Keep can be useful for quickly writing down ideas, reminders, short study notes and task lists.

Instead of keeping every small reminder in a long notebook, students can use short digital notes to keep track of things they need to remember.

5. Google Calendar

Google Calendar can help students organize classes, deadlines, examinations, assignments and personal activities.

A simple study schedule can make it easier to see what needs to be completed and when important dates are approaching.

6. Microsoft OneNote

Microsoft OneNote is a digital note-taking tool that can be useful for organizing notes into notebooks and sections.

It can be useful for students who prefer keeping different subjects or projects separated inside a digital notebook.

7. Canva

Canva can be useful when students need to create presentations, posters, visual assignments or other designs.

Its education resources also include tools and templates designed around classroom learning and creative projects.

[Canva Education](https://reference-url-citation.invalid/3)

8. Adobe Acrobat Reader

Many study materials are distributed as PDF files. A PDF reader can therefore be useful for opening and reading textbooks, notes, question papers and other documents.

Adobe Acrobat Reader is one option for working with PDF documents on mobile and desktop devices.

9. Google Sheets

Google Sheets can be useful for organizing information in tables. Students can use spreadsheets for study schedules, assignment tracking, simple calculations and project data.

It can be particularly helpful when a student needs to organize a large amount of information in rows and columns.

10. Google Slides

Google Slides can help students create presentations for projects, classroom activities and academic presentations.

Keeping presentations organized digitally also makes it easier to access them from different devices when needed.

How to Choose the Right Apps

You do not need to install every app listed in this guide. Choose tools according to the tasks you actually perform.

For example, a student who mainly needs notes and assignments might only need a note-taking app, cloud storage and a document editor. Another student working on presentations may benefit more from presentation and design tools.

Final Thoughts

Technology can make studying more organized, but apps themselves do not guarantee better results. The most useful approach is to choose a few reliable tools and build a simple routine around them.

Start with the tools that solve your biggest problems, keep your files organized and avoid installing unnecessary apps just because they are popular.
